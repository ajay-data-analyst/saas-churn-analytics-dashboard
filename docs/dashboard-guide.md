# Dashboard Guide

## Page 1: Executive Overview
Provides a high-level view of subscription, revenue, churn, retention, and customer health.

Main elements include MRR/ARR KPIs, churn and retention indicators, customer distribution, and the monthly new-business MRR trend.

The MRR area chart is sorted chronologically by Month-Year.

## Page 2: Customer Churn
Diagnoses churn across plan type, payment method, tenure, customer characteristics, and revenue churn.

The Count/Revenue bookmark views must remain because alternate-state visuals are referenced by the bookmarks.

## Page 3: Retention & Cohort
Analyzes retention by tenure segment and plan type.

Main elements include retention KPIs, retention heatmap, retention-by-tenure analysis, and Plan Type filtering.

Retention is based on the available snapshot and tenure segmentation, not longitudinal survival analysis.

## Page 4: Customer Value
Analyzes realized customer revenue and related value patterns.

Main elements include Avg Realized Revenue per Customer, Avg Realized Revenue - Churned, ARPU-related analysis, plan comparisons, and customer-value visuals.

Realized revenue is used instead of predictive LTV terminology.

## UX Features
- Page navigation
- Slicers
- Bookmark interactions
- Reset filters
- Drill-through/navigation where configured
- Consistent dark SaaS visual theme
