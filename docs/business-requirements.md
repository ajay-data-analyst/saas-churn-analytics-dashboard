# Business Requirements

## Project
SaaS Subscription & Churn Analytics Dashboard

## Objective
Create a professional Power BI analytics solution demonstrating SaaS subscription health analysis across churn, retention, revenue, tenure, cohort segmentation, customer risk, and feature adoption.

## Business Questions
- What is the current subscription and revenue health?
- Which customer segments show higher churn?
- How does churn vary by plan, payment method, tenure, and feature adoption?
- How does retention vary across tenure segments and plan types?
- Which customer segments require further retention analysis?
- How does realized revenue vary across plans and customer segments?

## Dashboard Scope
1. Executive Overview
2. Customer Churn
3. Retention & Cohort
4. Customer Value

## Key Metrics
MRR, ARR, Logo Churn Rate, Revenue Churn Rate, Retention Rate by Tenure Segment, Cohort MRR Momentum, ARPU, Avg Realized Revenue per Customer, Avg Realized Revenue - Churned, Avg Tenure Months, MoM Growth, YoY Growth, Rolling 12M MRR, and High Risk Customers.

## Data Constraint
The source is a snapshot-style customer dataset rather than a complete subscription event history. Metrics must therefore be interpreted within the available snapshot and start-cohort basis.

The project does not claim true longitudinal cohort survival, true Net Revenue Retention, predictive LTV, or real-client business impact.

## Portfolio Positioning
This is a portfolio project demonstrating technical and analytical capability. It is not presented as a deployed client solution.
